PLAN BEFORE YOU INVEST

Incentives and tax watch-outs

Potential incentives

The Playbook describes a 5% corporate tax rate for qualifying manufacturing activities, with investment thresholds and other conditions. It also describes a 5% personal tax incentive for qualifying knowledge workers.

These are conditional schemes, not automatic entitlements.

Cross-border tax risks

Review transfer pricing, permanent establishment exposure, intercompany transactions and different rules across Malaysia and Singapore. Get tailored advice before making commitments.

Sources: SMF Playbook, pp. 3, 6, 14–17. Verify latest eligibility with the responsible authorities.

PEOPLE & MOBILITY

Passes for staff and investors

Employment Pass (EP)

For expatriate roles, subject to category, salary and approval requirements. The Playbook notes revised salary requirements taking effect on 1 June 2026.

Professional Visit Pass (PVP)

For temporary professional assignments, such as consultancy, training or machinery installation, rather than regular long-term employment.

Investor Pass

For eligible foreign business travellers exploring or managing Malaysian investments, subject to applicable rules.

Source: SMF Playbook, appendix, pp. 27–29. Check the authorities for current pass rules.
